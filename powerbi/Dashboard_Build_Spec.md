# Power BI Dashboard Build Specification

## Page 1 — Inventory Executive Overview
Cards: Inventory Value, Stockout Rate %, Supplier OTIF %, Fill Rate %, Excess Stock Value, Average Lead Time.
Visuals: monthly demand vs purchases, stockout rate by warehouse, inventory value by category, supplier OTIF ranking.

## Page 2 — Stock Health & Working Capital
Excess stock by category, slow-moving inventory, days-of-inventory distribution, reorder-risk table, warehouse inventory matrix.

## Page 3 — Supplier Performance
Supplier OTIF, average lead time, fill rate, backorders by supplier, supplier-risk scatter.

## Page 4 — Demand, Replenishment & Risk
Demand/purchase/backorder trends, warehouse/category heatmap, reorder risk and Q4 seasonal pattern.

Slicers: Year, Warehouse, Region, Category, Supplier.
